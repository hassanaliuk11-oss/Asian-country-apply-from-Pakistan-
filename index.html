Complete online apply process — read before applying

Step 1 — Select a country: search the country name and open its “Work Visa / Permit” link first. Read the eligible work categories, nationality rules, employer sponsorship requirements and official fees.
Step 2 — Find a real vacancy: open “Official Jobs” or “Factory / Company Jobs”. Search job titles such as factory worker, production operator, packing, warehouse assistant, machine operator, welder, electrician, driver or technician. Apply through the employer’s official careers page where available.
Step 3 — Prepare documents: usually a valid passport, CV, recent photo, education/skill certificates and experience letters. The exact list varies by country and job. Do not upload sensitive documents to an unverified recruiter.
Step 4 — Submit the job application: create an account on the genuine portal, complete the form truthfully, upload requested documents and save the application/reference number. Attend interviews or skill/language tests if required.
Step 5 — Get a written offer: check employer name, salary, working hours, contract duration, overtime, accommodation, medical insurance, travel costs and deductions. Verify the company and recruiter independently.
Step 6 — Work permit / sponsorship: in many Asian countries, the employer must apply for approval or sponsor the work permit first. Follow the official country-specific instructions; a job application is not a visa approval.
Step 7 — Apply for the correct visa: after the required permit/approval, use the official immigration ministry, government visa portal or embassy/consulate instructions. Submit biometrics/medical/police documents only if required, pay official fees through authorised channels and keep receipts.
Step 8 — Before travelling from Pakistan: check current BEOE/OEC requirements, recruiter licence and whether a Protector of Emigrants process is required. Keep copies of the contract, visa, employer contacts and payment receipts.
Step 9 — Track the case: use the official application reference to check status. Do not book non-refundable travel until the visa and required employment approvals are confirmed.
Important: never work on a tourist/visit visa, never buy a fake job offer, and never trust “100% guaranteed visa” claims. Rules, fees and eligibility can change; verify the current instructions on the official source before paying.

How to understand the links

Work Visa / Permit: government immigration, labour or visa authority information. Some portals provide online filing; others explain the employer-sponsored process.

Official Jobs: government employment/recruitment portal where available.

Factory / Company Jobs: company careers or industrial-zone/company directory; check that the vacancy is on the employer’s real website.

Job Search: search helper only, not an official application form. Search results must be checked manually.

Overseas Recruitment: recruitment or migration authority; confirm that any agency is licensed and the job demand is valid.
